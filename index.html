DocuMint Privacy Policy

Last updated 3 October 2026

DocuMint collects nothing about you. It has no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising identifier, no account and no server. This page explains what that means in practice, and how you can check it yourself.

What DocuMint collects

Nothing. The App Store privacy label for DocuMint reads Data Not Collected, and that is the whole of it: no usage statistics, no device identifiers, no diagnostics, no contact details.

Where your documents go

Nowhere. Compressing, merging, splitting, scanning, signing, filling in a form and reading text from a scan all happen on your device. DocuMint opens your files where they already are, never keeps the only copy of anything, and never overwrites your original unless you choose to replace it.

There is no account

There is nothing to sign in to, nothing to register and no email address to give. DocuMint keeps no record of who you are, because it has nowhere to keep one.

There is no network

The app contains no networking code: no analytics SDK, no crash reporter, no cloud fallback. You don't have to take that on trust. Turn on Airplane Mode and every feature except buying Pro keeps working.

Buying DocuMint Pro

Purchases go through Apple's App Store. That is the one part of the app that uses the network, and Apple's own code does it. Apple handles the payment and tells DocuMint only whether a purchase is active. DocuMint never sees your name, your payment details or your Apple Account.

What stays on your device

To do its job, DocuMint keeps a few things in its own storage on your device. None of it is sent anywhere.

  • A search index. The names and text of documents you open, so you can search inside them from the library.
  • Spotlight. Document names and text are added to your device's Spotlight index, so the Search screen on your device can find them. iOS lets you turn this off in Settings.
  • Unfinished forms. What you type into a form before tapping Done is kept as a draft, so it survives the app closing. Drafts are left out of device backups and deleted when you finish or discard them.
  • Signatures. A signature you draw is kept in your device's Keychain and used only when you place it. It is not backed up and does not sync to your other devices.
  • Recent documents. References to the files you opened, so the library can show them again. These are references, not copies.
  • Files from the Share sheet. When you send a document to DocuMint from another app, a temporary copy is kept for up to a day so the app can open it, then deleted.
  • A count of finished jobs. DocuMint mentions Pro once, after your third finished job, and keeps this count to know when that is.
  • Trial reminder. If you ask to be reminded before a free trial ends, iOS schedules that reminder on your device.

If you back up your device, DocuMint's storage is part of that backup, apart from drafts and signatures. Backups are handled by Apple and controlled by you, not by DocuMint.

Crash reports

DocuMint has no crash reporter of its own. If you turn on sharing with app developers in your device's privacy settings, Apple may pass crash reports for DocuMint on to its developer. That is your choice, made in Settings, and you can change it there.

Deleting DocuMint

Deleting the app deletes everything it stored on your device. Your documents are not affected: they stay wherever you keep them.

Children

DocuMint collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If what DocuMint keeps or where it keeps it ever changes, this page changes with it, and the date at the top says when.
